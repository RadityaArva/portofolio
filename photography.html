THE STORY — A SINGLE FRAME

A Room Full of Flowers

There is more to this frame than the moment at its center.

Flowers and fabric draw my eye toward the couple, while the guests remain part of the scene around them. I photographed from within the gathering, keeping the canopy, the table, and the people in the same frame.

Leaves hang overhead and soft draping catches the light. I wanted to hold the whole room together in one photograph, with its details leading back to the quiet focus in the middle.
